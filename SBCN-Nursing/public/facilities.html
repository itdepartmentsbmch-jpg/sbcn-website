Nutrition Lab

Practical classes in diet planning and preparation.

Community Health Lab

OBG Lab

Child Health Lab

Paediatric and newborn care practice.

Computer Lab

The 715-bed parent hospital

Sri Balaji College of Nursing is a unit of Sri Balaji Medical College, Hospital & Research Institute. The parent hospital has 715 general teaching beds, state-of-the-art operation theatres, 60 intensive care beds and a 24-hour high-tech trauma care facility, giving students adequate clinical exposure throughout the course and during internship.

  • ICUs and casualty
  • Operation theatres
  • Dialysis
  • Cath lab
  • Oncology
  • All major & super specialities

Hostel

Hostel accommodation

Secure, comfortable and well-equipped hostels within the campus, designed to give students a homely environment that supports academic and personal growth. The hostels have wide corridors and neat, clean living quarters, a hygienic kitchen providing healthy and nutritious food, a recreation room for indoor games and reading, and an anteroom with a television.

Accommodation

  • Spacious rooms, AC and non-AC, with attached washroom
  • Individual bed, study table and storage for each student
  • Separate hostels for boys and girls

Security & anti-ragging

Prevention of ragging

Ragging or harassment in any form, physical or mental, is strictly prohibited in the institution, and anyone found to have indulged in such practices is liable to appropriate punishment. Ragging includes:

  • Forcing a student to perform any action against his or her will.
  • Physical violence of any kind against a student, and/or intimidation.

Ragging is also a cognisable offence under the State Act.

How to report

Lodge a written complaint with the Principal of the college. The complaint is placed before the Disciplinary Action Committee for investigation and suitable recommendation for action.
