About the College

Excellence in Nursing Education, Caring with Knowledge, Leading with Compassion.

Sri Balaji College of Nursing (SBCN), Renigunta, Tirupati, is a unit of Sri Balaji Medical College, Hospital & Research Institute (SBMCH&RI), established to meet the growing demand for skilled nursing professionals in the region.

The college is recognised by the Indian Nursing Council (INC), New Delhi and the A.P. Nurses & Midwives Council (APNMC), and is affiliated to Dr. NTR University of Health Sciences, Vijayawada. It is close to Tirupati city and the airport.

The college has modern infrastructure: comfortable, well-furnished departments, laboratories and classrooms, separate quarters for staff and nurses, up-to-date equipment, separate hostels for boys and girls, and recreation facilities on the premises.

The welfare of students is given prime importance at all times. Our induction programme takes a practical, interactive approach to teaching, and the college's strength lies in the quality of its faculty: experienced teachers who groom students and shape their careers.

The parent hospital has 715 general teaching beds with state-of-the-art operation theatres, 60 intensive care beds and a 24-hour high-tech trauma care facility, serving every section of the community. Free medical services are given to poor and needy patients. These teaching beds give students practical training through the course and during internship, making their learning complete and comprehensive.

Our vision

Vision

To be a centre of excellence in nursing education and research, fostering compassionate, competent and ethical nursing professionals who transform healthcare through evidence-based practice, leadership, and service to humanity with dignity and respect.

Quality policy

Quality Policy

Sri Balaji College of Nursing is committed to providing quality nursing education through competent faculty, advanced clinical training at its parent hospital, and continual improvement, to develop skilled and compassionate nurses who meet national and global healthcare standards.

Our mission

Mission

To prepare competent nursing professionals through value-based education, skilled clinical training at Sri Balaji Medical College, Hospital & Research Institute, and a commitment to community health and ethical practice.

Academic excellenceImpart quality nursing education integrating theoretical knowledge with clinical competence, aligned with Indian Nursing Council standards.
Holistic developmentNurture students with professional skills, ethical values, communication and critical thinking to meet global healthcare challenges.
Service & compassionInstil a spirit of care and empathy to serve individuals, families and communities, especially the underserved.
Research & innovationPromote evidence-based practice and nursing research that improves patient outcomes and healthcare delivery.
Professional leadershipDevelop graduates who uphold integrity, accountability and lifelong learning as leaders in clinical, educational and community settings.
