Admissions 2026-27

B.Sc Nursing – eligibility, admission process, documents, scholarships and hostel.

Admission to the B.Sc Nursing programme is through NEET / as per the admission guidelines of Dr. NTR University of Health Sciences, Vijayawada, in accordance with the norms of the Indian Nursing Council and the A.P. Nurses & Midwives Council.

Eligibility

Qualifying exam10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English
Minimum marks45% for General; 40% for SC/ST/BC
Age17 years by 31 December of the admission year
EntranceThrough NEET / as per Dr. NTRUHS admission guidelines
IntakeAs approved by INC & Dr. NTRUHS

Admission process

  1. Enquire

    Call toll-free or the Administrative Office for guidance on eligibility.

  2. Register

    Register for counselling as per the Dr. NTRUHS admission notification.

  3. Allotment

    Receive the seat allotment and pay the prescribed fee.

  4. Join

    Report to the college with original certificates for verification.

Documents

What to bring

Originals, plus two sets of self-attested photocopies.

  • SSC / 10th marks memo (proof of date of birth)
  • Intermediate / 10+2 marks memo and pass certificate
  • NEET score card / counselling allotment order
  • Study certificates
  • Transfer certificate and conduct certificate
  • Caste and income certificates, if applicable
  • Aadhaar card and passport-size photographs
  • Medical fitness certificate

Please confirm the final list with the college office, as requirements follow the Dr. NTRUHS notification for the year.

Student support

Scholarships, hostel & transport

Scholarships

The college supports eligible students in applying for Jnanabhumi Post-Matric Scholarships (SC/ST/BC/EBC/Minority). For the fee structure, please contact the college office.

Hostel

Separate hostels for boys and girls within the campus, with AC and non-AC rooms with attached washrooms, a warden, mess and study hall. Hostel details

Transport

College buses are provided for clinical postings and field visits.

Questions

Admission FAQs

Is NEET required for B.Sc Nursing?
Admission is through NEET / as per the admission guidelines issued by Dr. NTR University of Health Sciences for the year. Please check the current notification or call the college.
What are the minimum marks?
A minimum of 45% in 10+2 for General candidates and 40% for SC/ST/BC candidates, with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English.
I studied MPC. Can I join B.Sc Nursing?
No. B.Sc Nursing requires Physics, Chemistry and Biology along with English at the 10+2 level.
Are there hostels for both boys and girls?
Yes. The college has separate hostels for boys and girls within the campus.
Is scholarship support available?
Yes. The college supports eligible students in applying for Jnanabhumi Post-Matric Scholarships for SC/ST/BC/EBC/Minority students.
