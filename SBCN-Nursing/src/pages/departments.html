Department of Nursing Foundations

The starting point for every student. Core nursing skills are taught first in the Nursing Foundations lab and then at the bedside in the parent hospital.

Key focus areas

  • Basic nursing procedures
  • First aid
  • Health assessment
  • Ethics and professional values

Department of Medical-Surgical Nursing

Care of adults with medical and surgical conditions, with postings in the wards, ICUs, operation theatres, casualty, dialysis, cath lab and oncology.

Key focus areas

  • Adult Health Nursing I & II
  • Pharmacology and Pathology
  • Critical care
  • OT nursing

Department of Child Health Nursing

Care of newborns, children and adolescents, in the paediatric wards, NICU and PICU of the parent hospital.

Key focus areas

  • Paediatric nursing
  • Growth and development
  • IMNCI
  • NICU / PICU care

Department of Mental Health Nursing

Care of people with mental-health conditions, with emphasis on therapeutic communication and community support.

Key focus areas

  • Psychiatric nursing
  • Counselling
  • De-addiction
  • Community mental health

Department of Obstetrics & Gynaecological Nursing

Care of women through pregnancy, labour and after delivery, in the antenatal clinic, labour room and maternity wards.

Key focus areas

  • Midwifery
  • Antenatal, intranatal and postnatal care
  • Family welfare

Department of Community Health Nursing

Nursing beyond the hospital: rural and urban field practice, health camps in adopted villages, and participation in national health programmes.

Key focus areas

  • RCH (reproductive & child health)
  • Epidemiology
  • National health programmes
  • Rural and urban field practice

Department of Nursing Education & Administration

Prepares students to teach, supervise and manage, in the classroom and on the ward.

Key focus areas

  • Educational technology
  • Curriculum
  • Nursing management and leadership
  • Ward administration

Department of Nursing Research & Statistics

Builds research skills and supports students through their nursing research project in the fourth year.

Key focus areas

  • Research methodology
  • Biostatistics
  • Evidence-based practice
  • Project guidance
