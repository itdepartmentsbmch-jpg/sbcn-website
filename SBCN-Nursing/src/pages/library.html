Information & Learning Centre

About the library

The Sri Balaji College of Nursing library system comprises a departmental library in the college building and the Central Library of Sri Balaji Medical College, Hospital & Research Institute, together supporting teaching, research and extension programmes.

The college library is in the college building, with adequate seating and more than 3000 books of different titles. The Central Library of SBMCH is housed in three large halls and has more than 5579 books, 293 back volumes and 1248 titles of reference and textbooks, more than 50 print journals, and over 10,000 online journals through NTRMEDNET, DELNET, NDLI and IP online publishers.

The Information and Learning Centre provides year-round reference and information services. Staff help students develop effective search strategies, identify new resources, confirm citations, and use online databases and search engines.

Mission & vision

To provide resources and instructional material in support of the evolving curriculum, and to maintain access to modern information technology that meets the changing information needs of the community. The library acquires, manages and links physical and virtual information resources, and provides instruction so users can benefit fully from them, supporting lifelong learning and excellence in undergraduate, graduate and professional studies.

Rules

Library rules

Ragging is strictly prohibited in the library. The library is under CCTV surveillance.

General rules
  1. Every member must sign the register at the entrance.
  2. The library is open on all working days from 9 AM to 5 PM; holidays 9 AM to 4 PM.
  3. Members and associate members must have a library membership card to use the library.
  4. External faculty and students need prior permission and a temporary membership card.
  5. Strict silence must be observed. Bags, aprons and similar items are not allowed inside and must be left at the property counter. The library is not responsible for loss or damage of personal belongings.
  6. Eating, drinking, photography (including with mobile phones) and disturbing other members are not allowed.
  7. The librarian is the custodian of library resources and discipline, and may ask anyone breaking the rules to leave and report the matter to the Director, Dean and Principal.
  8. A member who misuses or loses a book must pay double its cost.
  9. Students must obtain a no-dues certificate from the library on completing the course.
  10. Private books must be kept on the shelves at the entrance. To use them inside, enter them in the register at the counter and show them when leaving.
Circulation rules
  1. The college library is mainly a reference library and follows an open-access system.
  2. Books are issued for a maximum of one week. Late returns are fined Rs. 10 per day per book. Defaced or damaged books must be replaced by the user.
  3. Issued material may be recalled at any time and must then be returned at once.
  4. An identity card / library card is compulsory for access.
  5. Do not fold the library card or alter its entries. Report a lost card to the librarian; a duplicate may be issued on application and payment of a fine. Cards are valid for the whole course.
  6. The library is for individual study; conversation and group discussion are not allowed.
Digital library & overdue charges
  1. Computers are for educational use only. Accessing unauthorised sites, playing games and similar misuse are prohibited and punishable.
  2. Users must not change computer settings.
  3. The library is not responsible for loss of valuables, laptops, phones or other devices in the reading rooms.
  4. Books must be returned on or before the due date; overdue fines are collected as per library policy.
