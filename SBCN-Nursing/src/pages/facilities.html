Security & anti-ragging

Prevention of ragging

Ragging or harassment in any form, physical or mental, is strictly prohibited in the institution, and anyone found to have indulged in such practices is liable to appropriate punishment. Ragging includes:

  • Forcing a student to perform any action against his or her will.
  • Physical violence of any kind against a student, and/or intimidation.

Ragging is also a cognisable offence under the State Act.

How to report

Lodge a written complaint with the Principal of the college. The complaint is placed before the Disciplinary Action Committee for investigation and suitable recommendation for action.
