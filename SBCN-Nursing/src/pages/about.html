Sri Balaji College of Nursing (SBCN), Renigunta, Tirupati, is a unit of Sri Balaji Medical College, Hospital & Research Institute (SBMCH&RI), established to meet the growing demand for skilled nursing professionals in the region.

The college is recognised by the Indian Nursing Council (INC), New Delhi and the A.P. Nurses & Midwives Council (APNMC), and is affiliated to {{university}}. It is close to Tirupati city and the airport.

The college has modern infrastructure: comfortable, well-furnished departments, laboratories and classrooms, separate quarters for staff and nurses, up-to-date equipment, separate hostels for boys and girls, and recreation facilities on the premises.

The welfare of students is given prime importance at all times. Our induction programme takes a practical, interactive approach to teaching, and the college's strength lies in the quality of its faculty: experienced teachers who groom students and shape their careers.

The parent hospital has 715 general teaching beds with state-of-the-art operation theatres, 60 intensive care beds and a 24-hour high-tech trauma care facility, serving every section of the community. Free medical services are given to poor and needy patients. These teaching beds give students practical training through the course and during internship, making their learning complete and comprehensive.
