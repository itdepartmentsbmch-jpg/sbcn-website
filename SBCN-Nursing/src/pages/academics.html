B.Sc Nursing curriculum

Every student shall undergo a period of certified study extending over four academic years divided into eight semesters (of six months each), from the date of commencement of study for the subjects of the nursing curriculum to the date of completion of the examination.

1. Eligibility to appear for the university examination

RequirementCriteria
AttendanceMinimum 80% in theory and 100% in practical / clinical in each subject
Internal assessmentMinimum 50% marks in theory and practical separately in each subject

3. Pass criteria

  • Pass in each subject: minimum 50% in university theory, 50% in university practical, and 50% aggregate in each subject.
  • No carry-over beyond the 6th semester: students must pass all 1st to 6th semester subjects before appearing for the 7th semester examination.
  • Grace marks: as per {{universityShort}} rules; maximum 5 marks in one subject if it helps the student pass the year.

4. Internship – Semester VIII

  • Duration: 22 weeks (1056 hours).
  • No theory examination.
  • Assessment: 50% internal (log book and competency assessment by the clinical supervisor) and 50% end-semester university practical examination.
  • Pass marks: 50% aggregate internal and 50% in the university examination. Students must complete all rotations.

5. Course completion

The entire course must be completed within 8 years from the date of admission.
