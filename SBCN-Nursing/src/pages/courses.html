Bachelor of Science in Nursing

The B.Sc Nursing programme prepares students to work as professional nurses in hospitals and communities, in India and abroad.

Every student undergoes a period of certified study extending over four academic years, divided into eight semesters of six months each. Teaching combines classroom lectures, seminars, hands-on practice in the skill labs, field visits, workshops and guest lectures, supported by the library, internet and Wi-Fi.

Clinical training takes place at the parent hospital, Sri Balaji Medical College, Hospital & Research Institute, with 715 teaching beds and all major and super specialities, and in rural and urban community postings. The eighth semester is a 22-week internship.

Subjects (INC revised syllabus 2021)

Communicative English, Applied Anatomy, Applied Physiology, Applied Sociology, Applied Psychology, Nursing Foundations, Applied Biochemistry, Applied Nutrition and Dietetics, Health / Nursing Informatics and Technology, Applied Microbiology and Infection Control including Safety, Pharmacology, Pathology and Genetics, Adult Health (Medical-Surgical) Nursing, Professionalism, Professional Values and Ethics, Child Health Nursing, Mental Health Nursing, Community Health Nursing, Educational Technology / Nursing Education, Introduction to Forensic Nursing and Indian Laws, Midwifery / Obstetrics and Gynaecology Nursing, Nursing Research and Statistics, Nursing Management and Leadership.

Admission details

Learning

How students learn

  • Digital classrooms, seminars and guest lectures
  • 8 INC-mandated laboratories and an Advanced Skill & Simulation Lab
  • OSCE, simulation-based training, BLS and mandatory modules
  • Clinical postings in the 715-bed parent hospital
  • Rural and urban community postings, health camps and school health programmes
  • Research guidance for the nursing research project in the 4th year
  • Faculty mentor for every 10–15 students; remedial coaching for those who need it
After the course

Career guidance

The college's placement cell arranges campus interviews with hospitals, and students receive coaching and guidance for:

  • NORCET (AIIMS nursing officer recruitment)
  • State staff nurse examinations
  • NCLEX, for nursing in the USA
  • IELTS / OET, for nursing abroad
  • Higher studies (M.Sc Nursing)
