About us

A nursing college within a medical college hospital

Sri Balaji College of Nursing (SBCN), Renigunta, Tirupati, is a unit of Sri Balaji Medical College, Hospital & Research Institute, established to meet the growing demand for skilled nursing professionals in the region.

The college is recognised by the Indian Nursing Council (INC), New Delhi and the A.P. Nurses & Midwives Council (APNMC), and is affiliated to {{university}}. It is close to Tirupati city and the airport.

Because the college is part of a medical college hospital, students get real-time exposure to ICUs, operation theatres, casualty, dialysis, the cath lab, oncology and every clinical department, taught by nursing faculty and trained alongside doctors and senior nurses.

Frequently asked

For anything else, call toll-free {{phone}} or the Administrative Office on {{phone3}}.

Is the college recognised?
Yes. Sri Balaji College of Nursing is recognised by the Indian Nursing Council (INC), New Delhi and the A.P. Nurses & Midwives Council (APNMC), and is affiliated to Dr. NTR University of Health Sciences, Vijayawada.
Which courses does SBCN offer?
The college offers the 4-year B.Sc Nursing degree (8 semesters) as per the Indian Nursing Council revised syllabus 2021, adopted by Dr. NTRUHS.
What is the eligibility for B.Sc Nursing?
10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English, with a minimum of 45% for General and 40% for SC/ST/BC candidates, and a minimum age of 17 years by 31 December of the admission year. Admission is through NEET / as per Dr. NTRUHS admission guidelines.
Where do students do their clinical training?
At the parent hospital, Sri Balaji Medical College, Hospital & Research Institute, which has 715 teaching beds, 60 intensive care beds and 24-hour trauma care, and in rural and urban community postings.
Are hostels available?
Yes. There are separate hostels for boys and girls within the campus, with AC and non-AC rooms with attached washrooms, Wi-Fi, 24/7 hot water, CCTV and round-the-clock wardens.
