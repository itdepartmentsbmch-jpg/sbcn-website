Questions

Admission FAQs

Is NEET required for B.Sc Nursing?
Admission is through NEET / as per the admission guidelines issued by Dr. NTR University of Health Sciences for the year. Please check the current notification or call the college.
What are the minimum marks?
A minimum of 45% in 10+2 for General candidates and 40% for SC/ST/BC candidates, with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English.
I studied MPC. Can I join B.Sc Nursing?
No. B.Sc Nursing requires Physics, Chemistry and Biology along with English at the 10+2 level.
Are there hostels for both boys and girls?
Yes. The college has separate hostels for boys and girls within the campus.
Is scholarship support available?
Yes. The college supports eligible students in applying for Jnanabhumi Post-Matric Scholarships for SC/ST/BC/EBC/Minority students.
